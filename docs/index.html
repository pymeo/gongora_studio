Gongora Studio

Desktop tool to manage, analyze and upload TikTok content.

Gongora Studio is a small, private desktop application used by the GONGORA music project to follow how its own social media content performs. It runs on the user's own computer and connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official APIs, after the account owner signs in with TikTok and approves access.

Sign in with TikTok

The account owner authorizes access through TikTok's official OAuth flow. The app never asks for or sees the TikTok password.

Analyze

Reads basic profile information, account statistics and the list of published videos with their public counters.

Upload

May, in the future, send content to TikTok through the official APIs, only when the account owner explicitly decides to.

Gongora Studio runs locally. Data obtained from TikTok is stored on the user's own computer, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service for details.
